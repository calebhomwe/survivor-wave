This audit asks one question: is v1.3.2 as premium as it can be? It answers with seven verified benchmark and design lenses (Survivor.io, Bloons TD 6, Clash of Clans, a survivor-like reference set of Vampire Survivors / Brotato / Archero / Halls of Torment / 20 Minutes Till Dawn, onboarding, difficulty design, and dopamine/feedback) plus two measurements: a headless play-test (16 bot runs, fresh profiles) and a static economy model. Every proposal below was re-checked against the live file by a verifier pass. Anything the code contradicted was corrected or dropped (see 5.9).
Line anchors are for HEAD `9fce191` (`survivor-wave.html`, 19,343 lines). The brief's 19,113-line anchors sit about +20 to +240 lines earlier, so re-grep before editing because the file shifts.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **Verdict: not yet premium, but the core run is closer than the rest of the game.** The cross-lens scorecard averages **2.40 / 5**. The mid/late run is genre-competitive (7 bosses land within +0.3 s of schedule, the longest reward gap is 32 s, 51 level-ups, 4 EVOs, 300–410 enemies on screen), while onboarding (1.50) and economy health (1.64) are the weakest dimensions.
2. The first session fails: **13 of 14 unassisted fresh-profile runs died at 36–107 s; the 14th froze in the chest loop at 104 s**, right after the fixed ELITE (70 s) and HORDE SURGE (80 s). No new player sees the 2:30 boss, a chest, an EVO or the tower layer. CASUAL did not outlast NORMAL for either bot (79.9 vs 85.2 s; 84.2 vs 106.9 s).
3. Trust-breaking defects sit on the core reward paths. A chest re-opens every frame (soft-lock, or 159 free upgrades from one chest). Coins pay 0 Silver (`cv*b31GoldMult()`, 4928/15623). The 7-boss gauntlet and its ~950 Silver + 140 oil fire only in the first run per page load. TRY AGAIN skips the reset chain (14328 vs 19335). KEEP GOING pays a win twice and spawns negative-HP enemies. Chapters 14–17 can never unlock (3052). The sign-in pays 0 after day 7, and the daily chest says +150 while paying 600.
4. The build does not carry the run: a 0.8% random **Bomb Pickup did 67% of all damage**, while 15 towers did **0.3%**.
5. The meta is shallow and leaky. Every persistent sink (68,089 Silver) is maxed in **~3.8 h** at 3k/run. In-run towers drain the same bank. Stacked multipliers reach x4.85, and Nightmare/Peril/NG+ pay more than they cost.
6. **Lever 1 — Fix trust first (all S effort):** chest loop, coin value, per-run resets, the againBtn/IRONMAN/NG+ wrappers, endless double-pay, labels (P035, P046, P017, P047, P090).
7. **Lever 2 — A first 10 minutes that sells the game:** BOOT CAMP for the first 3 runs, a first level-up in 5 s or less, a guaranteed crate at 40–45 s, and a death screen that routes to a concrete upgrade (P001–P006).
8. **Lever 3 — Make the build matter:** cap the bomb, scale towers with the run, split an in-run FIELD BUDGET from the Silver bank, and tell the gauntlet through a HUD tracker (P019, P020, P048, P018).
9. **Lever 4 — Give players a reason to return tomorrow and next week:** a looping sign-in, DAILY ORDERS, KINGDOM GAMES weekly, a free CAMPAIGN SEASON, run-counted construction (P066, P067, P075, P076, P052).
10. **Lever 5 — A long, readable ladder past 4 hours:** unlock ch14–17, real difficulty tiers leading to a THREAT ladder with medals, a collection archive, and long sinks (FIELD GEAR, doctrine tree) that never touch real money (P049, P080, P086, P073, P074, P057).

## 3. Measured pacing & economy

### 3.1 Play-test method (M-PT)

Headless Playwright 1.56 + Chromium served over 127.0.0.1. Every run starts from a FRESH profile (bank 80 Silver, no Armory/Kingdom). Level-ups auto-pick the first card, chests are clicked through, the bot builds the cheapest affordable tower near the hero, buys the cheapest open oil path step and presses E near inactive stations. Bot tiers: NAIVE (WASD 1.2 s cycle, r1–r7), KITE (steer away from enemies and bullets, drift to gems, k1–k7), KITE+RESCUE (HP reset to 60% below 12% HP, logged as a "would-die" event, plus a chest-loop workaround). KITE+RESCUE is the only way a fresh bot reaches 10:00, so **k1f** (ch1 NORMAL, 600 s WIN) is the canonical timeline and k10f is ch10 NORMAL with rescue (died 146.7 s). Headless fps 9–59 (median ~40); dt is clamped to 0.05, so game time lags wall time below 20 fps. Caveats: the bots do not aim, dash or ult; n=1 per cell; only 50g Slings were built (cheapest-first); chapters 5/10/17 were force-selected; k1f needed 22 HP rescues, so its HP numbers show pressure rather than a survivable run.

### 3.12 Play-test findings

- **CRITICAL — the chest re-opens endlessly.** The pickup sets `pk.dead=true`, calls `openChest()` and `return`s before `sweep(pickups,pk=>!pk.dead)` (~4880-4946 vs 4972), and the loop never checks `pk.dead`. Repro (chestrepro.py): 1 chest + 8 "Nice!" clicks = `__opens` 1..8. k7 froze at 104.3 s for 350 s of wall time. k1r took 159 opens from one elite chest (every weapon and passive Lv5, maxHp 1510 from Feast). The k1f workaround removed 10 looping chests in 10 min, so it fires on essentially every chest. → P035
- **CRITICAL — a fresh profile cannot survive 70–110 s.** All 14 unassisted runs died: ch1 NORMAL 85.2/106.9 s, CASUAL 79.9/84.2, NIGHTMARE 50.8/77.6, ch5 47.5/83.3, ch10 52.1/51.5, ch17 39.9/35.8, ch1 no-towers 64.3/(chest lock). The first k1f rescue came at 80.8 s, right after "HORDE SURGE!" at 80.1 s, and k1f needed 22 rescues. → P001, P002, P003, P008
- **MAJOR — CASUAL is not measurably easier.** Survival naive casual 79.9 < normal 85.2 > nightmare 50.8; kite 84.2 < 106.9 > 77.6. The 0.75x HP / 0.9x spawn discount is swamped by the fixed 70 s elite + 80 s horde. NIGHTMARE is readable (3.4x the enemies at 60 s). → P080
- **MAJOR — in-run Silver is ~0 for 90 s, so the persistent bank funds towers.** One 50g Sling at t=1, the next at 88 s, a 120g tower at 157 s, a 60g station at 315 s (in range 11 times before 128 s with 3–31 Silver). The coin drop is 11% × coinVal(3) and pays ~0. validSpot checks `gold<costOf(type)`, and gameOver adds gEarn to the same `gold`. → P046, P048
- **MAJOR — towers barely register.** 15 Slings = 0.3% of damage (324–8,899 dmg and 0–7 pops per tower over 10 min). Early they matter more (r1 at 85 s: towers 2,355 of ~13k non-bomb damage, ~18%). Removing towers: naive 64.3 vs 85.2 s; kite 104.3+ vs 106.9 s. → P020
- **MAJOR — the Bomb Pickup dominates:** map-wide 250+30·difficulty to every enemy (~4915), 67% of the canonical ledger. → P019
- **MAJOR — the early game is slow to reward.** First level-up at 14–23 s on ch1 (genre ~3–5 s) and 20.7 s / 45.7 s on ch10. 0 chests in the first 90 s of all 16 runs (the first elite is at nextElite=70), the first oil rush at ~111 s, and xp still 1/5 at 10 s with 12 enemies on the field. → P002, P003
- **MINOR (positive shape) — no dead zone over 60 s** once past the wall: the longest gap is 32.0 s, the median level-up interval 9.1 s, Lv26 at 174 s and Lv52 at 583 s, matching the pace keeper `expL=min(1+t/16, 26+t/40)`.
- **POSITIVE — the gauntlet is punctual:** all 7 bosses land within +0.3 s. Elites at 70, 120, 170, 220, 270, 305, 340, 375, 410, 445, 480, 515, 550, 585; hordes at 80, 220, 290, 360, 430, 500, 570.
- **POSITIVE — the post-wall 10-minute arc is Survivor.io-shaped:** 4 EVOs (RPG 225 s, Cryo 459, Kunai 536, Molotov 583), 10 chests, 6 oil rushes, a full 15-tower build by 227 s, 31 oil upgrades, Lv52, 16,748 kills, 300–410 enemies late.
- **MINOR — oil never stockpiles:** it hovers at 1–30 against a cap of 100–355. 31 steps were bought (30×30 + 1×60), with ≈888 oil income over 600 s. The +5 cap per level is irrelevant. → P025, P026, P027
- **MINOR — wall-clock timers:** oil regen (setInterval 500 ms, ~17005), oil rush (setInterval 1000 ms, ~18800) and station heal. Rushes came 81–105 game-seconds apart against a 110 s design minimum when fps dipped to 10. → P024
- **MINOR — late chapters are stat checks:** ch10/ch17 open with 3–4 NEW FOE banners (good), but a fresh profile dies at 36–52 s with 14–16 kills vs 92–126 on field at 30 s (chHP 2.41x / 4.36x, spawn 1.54x / 1.96x). → P081, P088

## 6. Top 12 highest-leverage changes (retention impact ÷ effort)

1. **P035 Stop the chest re-open soft-lock** (S) — Every chest either freezes the run or grants 159 free upgrades. It is the genre's core reward, blocks every other in-run measurement, and is a one-line loop guard. *→ #4 Dopamine moments 2.71, #2 In-run pacing 3.07.*
2. **P046 Restore coin value** (S) — A one-expression fix (`cv*(1+mult)`) turns "+0 GOLD" into a visible micro-reward, revives GOLD HOARD and funds early towers. *→ #6 Economy health 1.64.*
3. **P017 Per-run reset integrity** (S) — Restores the 7-boss gauntlet and ~950 Silver + 140 oil for every retry, and makes TRY AGAIN, IRONMAN and NG+ do what they say. *→ #6 Economy health 1.64, #9 Difficulty ladder 2.07.*
4. **P001 BOOT CAMP opening** (S) — Moves the first-session wall from 1:20 to past the 2:30 boss for the only players who churn instantly, touching just two timers and a ramp. *→ #1 Onboarding & first session 1.50.*
5. **P002 First level-up in 5 s or less** (S) — Closes the 17 s silent opening with an xpNext literal, two threshold overrides and a gem ring, bringing the genre's first dopamine hit into the first 5 seconds. *→ #1 Onboarding & first session 1.50.*
6. **P066 A sign-in calendar that keeps paying** (S) — The only daily hook dies on day 8; restarting the week, with a streak and a tomorrow preview, is a single branch change. *→ #7 Retention (daily/weekly) 2.57.*
7. **P049 Unlock chapters 13–17 + MARCH ON** (S) — Four finished biomes, 8 stars and ~4,900 Silver open with one comparison change; MARCH ON makes "one more chapter" a single tap. *→ #8 Long-term goals 2.00.*

9. **P006 Death screen "What next"** (M) — The most-seen screen for new players becomes a near-miss line plus a one-tap affordable upgrade and routes into the meta game. *→ #1 Onboarding & first session 1.50.*
